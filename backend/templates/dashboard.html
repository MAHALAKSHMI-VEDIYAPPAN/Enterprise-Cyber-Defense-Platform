Welcome to ECDP

The Enterprise Cyber Defense Platform (ECDP) is a centralized cybersecurity platform that brings asset management, vulnerability assessment, threat intelligence, CVE analysis, and incident response together within a unified security environment.

It is designed to support practical Security Operations Center (SOC) workflows and provide security analysts with a centralized platform for security monitoring and analysis.
